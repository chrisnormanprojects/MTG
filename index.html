What counts as a UK price?

Only an available offer explicitly recorded as English from a UK seller is labelled “UK offer”. Postage, import costs and condition may affect what you pay. These offers require a connected source.

What is the EU guide price?

Cardmarket publishes a daily Europe-wide lowest asking price. It does not filter to English cards or UK sellers. Any pounds conversion shown here is indicative, not a UK shop quote.
